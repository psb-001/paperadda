Delete Your Data

Last updated: 26 September 2026

You can ask us to erase the data you have sent us at any time, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. There is no account to close, because the student app has no sign-in.

What we can delete

  • Your content requests (the branch, year, type, title, details and status of each request).
  • Your feedback messages and any email address you chose to share with us.

We do not hold your name, phone number, address, photos or payment details, so there is nothing else to delete.

How to request deletion

  1. Email chillmaaryaaar@proton.me from the same email address you used (if you provided one).
  2. Tell us you want your data erased. If you sent a request or feedback in the app, include the anonymous request ID shown in the app so we can find the right records.
  3. We will verify the request, then permanently delete the records from our live database and from backups on our normal backup rotation.

We aim to complete verified erasure requests within 30 days. We will confirm by email once done.

Other data requests

The same email address can be used to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to raise a grievance. See the Privacy Policy for the full list of your rights.
