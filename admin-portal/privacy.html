Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Applies to the PaperAdda Android app and this website

In plain language: using the PaperAdda student app requires no account and no personal details. If you submit a content request or feedback, we store only what you choose to send (plus a random anonymous ID stored on your device) so we can reply and improve. We never sell your data.

1. Who is responsible

PaperAdda ("we", "us") operates the PaperAdda Android application and this website. We are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here and are responsible for following the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

Contact for any privacy question, request or grievance: chillmaaryaaar@proton.me.

2. What we collect

  • Catalog data (no personal data). Subjects, question papers and notes published by our administrators.
  • Content requests. The branch, year, type, title and details you type, the status of the request, and a randomly generated identifier (a UUID) created on your device. The UUID is not linked to your name, phone number, email or any account.
  • Feedback. The category and message you type. Email is optional and only collected if you enter it so that we can reply. We also record the app version and platform (e.g. "1.0", "Android") that sent the message.
  • Admin accounts (this website). When an administrator signs in we store their email address, a display name, an account role, and authentication records (password hashes, session tokens and sign-in events).
  • Technical data seen by our hosting providers. Our infrastructure providers (Supabase for the database, authentication and file storage; Cloudflare for this website) may process IP addresses, device information, request logs and timestamps for delivery, security and abuse prevention.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, sensitive personal data (such as Aadhaar, PAN, bank or payment details, health data, or full birth dates).

3. Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • Consent: when you send a content request or feedback, you consent to us processing that message for the purpose of fulfilling your request, replying to you, and improving the app. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing us to delete your message.
  • Performance of a contract / legitimate use: delivering the app, authenticating administrators, securing the service, and preventing abuse.
  • Legal obligation: retaining records where required by applicable law.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not share it for marketing.

4. Cookies and local storage

The student app does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The admin sign-in page stores your session in your browser's localStorage (key paperadda-admin-session) so you stay signed in. This is strictly necessary for authentication and is removed when you log out or clear site data. We do not run third-party marketing or tracking cookies on this website.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who process it on our instructions, and only to operate the service: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) and Cloudflare (website hosting and security). We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data if required by a valid court order or applicable law.

6. How long we keep it

  • Content requests: kept while open and for up to 12 months afterwards, then deleted.
  • Feedback: kept until the message is resolved or you ask us to delete it, and no longer than 12 months after that.
  • Admin accounts: kept while the person is part of the team; sign-in and security logs are kept for up to 12 months.
  • Backups: deleted on our backup rotation (within 30 days).

7. Your rights (DPDP Act)

Wherever the DPDP Act applies to you, you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and ask for a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • have your data erased when it is no longer needed;
  • withdraw consent, on notice to us;
  • file a grievance with us, and receive a response within the time required by the DPDP Act.

To exercise any of these rights, email chillmaaryaaar@proton.me from the same email you used (if you gave one) and, for app requests, include the anonymous request ID shown in the app so we can find it. We verify the request before deleting anything, to avoid removing someone else's data. See also the Delete your data page.

8. Security

Data is stored in a managed cloud database. Access to the database is restricted to authorised administrators through authenticated, role-checked access; end-user tables cannot be read or written directly from the app. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send us anything highly sensitive.

9. Children

PaperAdda is intended for students who are at least 13 years old, or who use it with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below 13; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, note the change in the app. Continuing to use the service after an update means the updated policy applies from that date.
